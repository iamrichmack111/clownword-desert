# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project respectful, welcoming, and free from harassment for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code.
- Welcome beginners and educational contributors.
- Avoid insulting, discriminatory, threatening, or sexualized language.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and interactions that violate these expectations. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.
